# FARTWATER — S1E1

## “Atlanta: Receipts in the Peach State”

*(Trash-doc reality tone — cameras always a half-second late, confessionals cut in like sucker punches, lower-thirds that lie.)*

### COLD OPEN — *“Headliner Energy”*

Dusk at the Georgia State Fair parking lot: ferris wheel lights twitch on like a migraine, cotton-candy air, gravel coughing under bald tires. The convoy limps into frame:

* **The main tour bus**: vinyl wrap faded to the color of old gum, a FartWATER bottle the size of a toddler bolted to the roof at an angle that says “code violation.”
* **Ludacris’s “lux” bus**: slightly cleaner, mismatched chrome rims, an LED marquee in the front window scrolling **HEADLINER** in a font that’s doing its best.
* **Will & Soulja’s Chaos Bus**: spray-painted, livestream ring lights suction-cupped to the glass like jellyfish.
* **The FartWATER Van**: Red Bull Girls knockoff, roof bottle zip-tied on, trunk latch defeated by faith alone.

**Moses Gunner** hits the ground first: vape clenched between molars, **Bang Energy** can in each hand like dumbbells. He shoulders a messenger bag puking receipts and a plastic case on a lanyard — **FartLedger™** — thumping his chest. He looks at the camera the way a raccoon looks at a porch light.

> **Moses (confessional, breathy, vape plume):**
> “Receipts are liquidity. I’m the firewall and the fire. I don’t sleep — I **chug**. This tour is in compliance with destiny.”

**Ja Rule** thuds out second, dragging the **FartWATER Finance BTC ATM** on a squealing dolly. The machine prints a receipt the second it smells air:
**APR: 999%**
**AUTO SLIPPAGE: 80%**
**NOTE: YOU BOUGHT HIGH, SOLD LOW. SORRY.**

**Bow Wow** unfolds from the bus in **FartWATER pajamas** — grease veteran, eyes locked on the food court like it owes him child support. He’s got a plastic fork behind one ear and a dental emergency waiting to happen.

**Ludacris** emerges in aviators despite the sun tapping out. He lifts his chin like it’s a trophy.

> **Luda (confessional, grim smile):**
> “ATL is home. That’s headline jurisdiction. I don’t **audition** for a fair. I bless it.”

**Will Smith** slides out in balanced dad-energy, already composing a moral. **Soulja Boy** slams the door open with a GoPro and a lie.

> **Soulja:** “**First rapper at the fair.** No cap.”
> **Will (to camera, earnestly):** “Family is liquidity. Even cotton candy needs community.”

The **FartWATER Girls** — **Crystal** (wannabe influencer), **Diamond** (bag-logic accountant), **Peaches** (gremlin with lip gloss) — materialize from nowhere. Crystal is live, Diamond is already selling, Peaches has theft in her eyes.

Cut to title on a crooked light pole banner:
**ATLANTA — RECEIPTS IN THE PEACH STATE**

## WEEK 1 — *The Bar-S Glizzy Hustle (Airdrop Mayhem)*

Paperwork is a suggestion. Moses decides a **Waffle House receipt** is a permit if you laminate it. He slaps it on a clipboard and smiles like the sun owes him rent. The fair manager looks at the clipboard, at Moses, at the vape, and surrenders to fate.

The **shopping cart grill** gets born behind the livestock barn: a rusted cart on its side, a propane tank mummified in duct tape, a heating element zip-tied like a dare. Bow Wow christens it with a Bar-S hotdog that hits the grate and sighs.

**Banner (too small, wrong font):**
**BAR-S HOTDOG HUSTLE**
**WIN $50 $FRTWTR AIRDROP + A CASE OF FARTWATER™**
**10 MINUTES. DON’T DIE.**

Moses holds a bullhorn like a hostage. “Scan the QR to register — airdrop on completion.” The QR leads to **/loans/** half the time and **/bustasfinance/** (451) the other half. The ATM spits a commentary track:

* **AUTO 20% FEE APPLIED**
* **YOU BOUGHT HIGH, SOLD LOW. SORRY.**
* **AUTO SLIPPAGE: 80%** *(the printer squeals; a kid flinches)*

**Contestants** line up like a casting call from Craigslist:
Two fat white guys with cutoff tees and the confidence of county champions; one fat Black guy already unbuckling his belt… ceremonially; one fat Hispanic dude psyching himself with nods to an invisible coach; one **little Asian guy** the crowd starts betting on like a secret weapon. Bow Wow slides in at the end in pajamas.

> **Moses:** “You’re not eligible, Bow.”
> **Bow:** “I’m brand.”
> **Diamond (to camera, smirking):** “Internal wallet conflict.”

**Crystal** starts a live poll: “Winner gets **$50 in $FRTWTR** and a *case* of FartWATER. Loser gets content.” **Peaches** lays out stacks of lukewarm bottles that sweat like guilt.

The **grill** spits little black commas of ash. Bow flips dogs with a hotel room key. The timer is a stolen egg timer with nail polish numbers.

“**Ten minutes!**” Moses screams. The horn honks like it’s dying.

Meat vanishes. The glizzies are **boiled to gray** then scarred on the cart until they look like they saw some things. Bow grabs one, bites, and **chips a tooth** on something that absolutely used to be part of an animal but not **this** animal. He spits into a FartWATER cup and grins with a bright red shard.

> **Bow (confessional, defiant):**
> “You catch a bone, you catch a blessing. That’s **brand**.”

Nine minutes stretch like summer detention. The crowd starts a chant that sounds like **PAY! RULE! PAY! RULE!**, which Ja pretends is encouragement and not a threat. The ATM prints:

* **INSUFFICIENT CLOUT**
* **AIRDROP QUEUED (PROBABLY)**
* **GAS TOO HIGH, TRY WALKING**

The **little Asian guy** goes robotic and **crushes nine**. He stands like a field of wheat in a stiff breeze. The horn wheezes its last. The crowd surges; **Ja** shoves **$50** into the winner’s palm like he’s paying a debt to the universe.

> **Crystal (to camera):** “Tag me if you threw up.”

Bow swallows a groan and two dogs that no longer qualify as food. **Luda** watches from a folding chair with the air of a monarch forced to view peasant games.

> **Luda (confessional, sincere):**
> “That was a headline-caliber opener.”

The ATM spits one more:
**LOSS REALIZED.**
**80% SLIPPAGE.**

## WEEK 2 — *City Antics, Soft Power, and Glizzy Gravy*

Morning at **Centennial Olympic Park**. **Busta Rhymes** has stolen a sidewalk like a startup steals a user base. He tapes up foam boards in a pie chart that is **blank** but labeled **CONSENSUS**. He wires a power strip into a lamp post like the city is a wall socket.

> **Busta (pitch cadence, eyes lit):**
> “**Hydration throughput** is a yield instrument. You don’t drink water — you **stake** it.”

He slaps QR stickers onto folding handouts. They flutter in the humidity, peel at the corners, and reroute to **/loans/** or 451 like they’re allergic to the truth.

**Will & Soulja** attempt a fountain dance. Soulja declares, “**First rapper to master the fountain**,” then slips, then pivots: “**First rapper to crash a fountain!**” Will smiles into the wind and says, “Family.”

**Underground Atlanta** becomes **Ludacris’s personal parade**. He walks with a megaphone shouting his own walk-through: “**Headliner entering! Headliner passing!**” Nobody cares. A tourist asks if he’s a wax figure. He narrates anyway.

**Bow Wow** discovers **glizzy gravy** like a prophet discovers a mountain. He steals the hotdog water from the night before — a gray tea that smells like a dare — and moves into **R&D**. On a plastic folding table, he dumps cornstarch into a pot and stirs with a spatula stolen from a churro cart.

He ladles the **Glizzy Gravy™** over fries, biscuits, a churro, a napkin. It **coats** with the mood of a bad decision.

> **Bow (barker voice, smeared smile):**
> “**Official utility sauce of FartWATER.** It’s liquidity you can lick.”

**Nelly** hovers like a priest at a barbeque.

> **Nelly (sermon whisper, rhythmic):**
> “**Coupons are bonds.** Hotdogs are **stable**. Gravy… gravy **diversifies the peg**.”

**FartLedger™** hangs on **Moses’s** chest like a baptism he gave himself. **Peaches** notices and grins.

> **Peaches (confessional, lip gloss physics):**
> “If I hold the chain, I’m CFO. That’s just math.”

The ATM, dragged down a block, has opinions:

* **MULTI-SIG PENDING — PLEASE WAIT**
* **APR TOO HIGH — SEE MOSES**
* **80% SLIPPAGE** *(a jogger drops her smoothie like a gun went off)*

Night falls. The crew has built a **religion out of trash** and **Bow’s** tongue is numb.

## WEEK 3 — *The Georgia State Fair Show (Legit Gig, Trash Execution)*

Load-in is a scavenger hunt. **Luda** demands the only **wireless mic**, a **fog machine**, **two towels**, and a **towel guy**. **Ja** chains the **ATM** stage-left like it’s a bronze bust of himself. **Busta** plugs a kids’ projector into his pie chart; it displays **NO SIGNAL** like a threat.

**Will & Soulja** rehearse a “**brotherhood is liquidity**” interlude while a goat eats one of their set lists.

The **FartWATER Girls** flank the wings:

* **Crystal** is a ring light with legs.
* **Diamond** has a fanny pack that’s actually a **merchant services department**.
* **Peaches** is **aiming at the FartLedger™** like a magpie.

Showtime misfires.

**Luda** sprints on first and claims the air like it’s land. “**ATL — your **headliner** is here!**” The crowd — half curious, half corn dog — murmurs like a stomach.

**Busta** interrupts a chorus with a **blank pie chart** and the shout **“CONSENSUS ACHIEVED!”** A confetti cannon **misfires** and coughs a single streamer that lands on an old man’s ear and **stays** there like a marriage.

**Will & Soulja** slide into a **sermon interlude** about **brotherhood** and **drinking water as family**. Soulja’s stream **dies**. The QR graphic on the jumbotron **autoredirects** to **/bustasfinance/** and displays **451: Unavailable for Legal Reasons**. Will soldiers on like the message is the message.

**Ja** finds a mark. A local in a Braves cap lets him “**convert $500 to $FRTWTR**” for the **experience**. The **ATM** **coughs**, **groans**, then **vomits receipts** like slot machine confetti over three rows of fair people.

Receipts rain, mean:

* **AUTO 20% FEE APPLIED**
* **PAYMENT IN ARREARS**
* **LEDGER NOT FOUND** *(Moses slaps his chest: “Found.”)*
* **GAS TOO HIGH, TRY WALKING**
* **80% SLIPPAGE!** *(the printer screams; babies cry; a teen yells “that’s my dad’s password!” for reasons we never learn)*

**Backstage**, reality is a clown car. **Crystal** tries for a “**VIP confessional**” with **Luda**. **Diamond** blocks: “I manage access.” **Peaches** rifles **Bow’s** backpack for dental wax, finds the **FartLedger™** case, and smiles like felony.

> **Sponsor Rep (hissing to Moses):**
> “This… this isn’t the deck we agreed on.”
> **Moses (confessional, serene):**
> “It’s not chaos. It’s **yield**.”

**Finale**: Luda buried in receipts yelling **“ATL — your headliner loves you!”** The crowd finally finds rhythm: **ALWAYS! PAY! RULE!** The ATM prints on beat:

**YIELD: UNCLEAR.**

## WEEK 4 — *Diners, Racism, Reckonings, and the Van Chase*

**Denny’s.** 2:07 AM. Fluorescent hum like a dental drill. The crew is a **migration**; the hostess gives them a smile so tight it squeaks. They’re seated in a corner booth under a dust-frosted potted plant.

**Ja** drags the **ATM** to the aisle.
**Moses** sets down a **tray of Bang** like communion.
**Bow** politely asks a server: “Do y’all have… **hotdog water**?”

The **manager** arrives with I-read-the-news energy. “We reserve the right to refuse—”

It never gets to **service**. They’re on the sidewalk before the first pancake can dream of a plate.

**Bow Wow** detonates.

> **Bow (confessional, eyes wet, tooth chipped like a badge):**
> “**Pancakes are real and can be eaten. That’s utility. That’s value.** You gon’ refuse **value**? That’s un-American.”

**Moses** folds the **check** into his pocket like it’s a **T-bill**. “Treasury. That’s **treasury**,” he whispers to the camera, vape crackling.

**Waffle House.** 2:53 AM. Neon buzz, grease perfume, a fight already brewing at the counter — two strangers in **work boots** debating who stole whose **night**. The crew slides into a booth like they were born in one.

**Soulja** leans over a stranger’s plate: “**First rapper to eat your hashbrowns.**” The stranger blinks slow. **Will** spreads his hands: “Brothers. We are brothers. Hydrate with me.”

**Crystal** films a selfie in front of a **sizzling argument**. “We out here with **community**,” she purrs, tagging **#FartWATERFamily** while a ketchup bottle becomes a projectile behind her head.

**Moses** leans over the counter at a **line cook** with a face carved from cigarette breaks. “I can get you on **$FRTWTR airdrops**. We slap QRs on the menu, you get **lifetime liquidity.**”

The **spatula** flies like a warning shot, claps the wall and falls into a puddle of its own reflection.

> **Moses (confessional, vape grin):**
> “Negotiation is just **receipts** with feelings.”

**IHOP.** 3:38 AM. Bow demands **premium pancakes** like a litigant. He stacks **BOGO coupons** into a little skyline on the table. **Nelly** holds court in the parking lot, preacher cadence under a parking-lot moon.

> **Nelly (sermon, a rhythm you could ride to work):**
> “**Coupons are bonds.** Pancakes are **stable**. You stake a short stack, you stake your neighbor. This is **finance**.”

**Soulja** stares into his phone lens.

> “**First rapper to flip a short stack into a bond — facts.**”

**Luda** in a corner booth, sunglasses on, mood at half-mast.

> **Luda (confessional, tight):**
> “This is my city and I’m at **IHOP**. I should be at **Mercedes-Benz**. I should be **on a coin**— wait, we **have** a coin. And I’m headliner.”

**The staff** at IHOP… **let them in**. They **serve** them. They **smile**. They pour coffee like a promise and look **nobody** up and down. Bow eats pancakes so fast Nelly calls it **liquidity**. Moses staples coupons to his **receipt bible** and calls it **RWA** under his breath, not knowing he just named a war he’ll lose in Chicago.

> **Will (soft):**
> “This is the **least racist 24/7 diner in America**.”
> **Peaches (confessional, bored and beautiful):**
> “IHOP let us be **ugly** and still fed us. That’s governance.”

**Back in the parking lot**, the **FartLedger™** glints. **Peaches** lunges. Fingers on chain, vape in teeth, **Moses** screams with a voice that sounds like a photocopier dying.

Bang can falls. Syrup bottle falls. **Receipts** explode into the air like confetti in a mall for ghosts. The FartWATER Van’s roof bottle wobbles and points at heaven like an accusation.

**Sirens** slide into the mix like a DJ nobody booked.

> **Moses (hiss, to everyone at once):**
> “**In the van. In the van. Right now.**”

They **pile** in: **Ja** cradling the **ATM**, **Bow** with pockets full of **mustard packets** like they’re gold teeth, **Busta** hugging a power strip like a toddler, **Nelly** with a fist of **BOGO** like rosaries, **Diamond** already selling something that doesn’t exist, **Crystal** live, **Peaches** smiling at the ledger she nearly stole.

The **FartWATER Van** pulls out with all the grace of a roller skate on gravel. The **ATM** in back prints like a **ticker tape machine** caught in a hurricane.

* **VEHICLE IMPACT FEE**
* **ROUTE SURCHARGE: FAIRGROUNDS**
* **PROJECTILE PERMIT: NOT FOUND**
* **80% SLIPPAGE! 80% SLIPPAGE! 80% SLIPPAGE!**

Blue-and-reds **wash** the inside of the van. **Moses** kills a vape, opens another Bang, mutters “**permits**” like it’s a safe word.

> **Luda (from his bus, on speaker, somehow):**
> “Headliner immunity!”

The **FartWATER Van** coughs smoke like a lie. It **dies** mid-turn under the gaze of a streetlight that’s seen worse but not **this**.

**Tow truck** arrives for the **ATM** first. It gets **hauled separately**, still **printing**, chained to the tow arm like a dog that doesn’t know who it belongs to. The last receipt sticks to the inside of the window like a love note.

* **RWA: LATER** *(a prophecy nobody asked for)*
* **YIELD: UNDECIDED**
* **80% SLIPPAGE**

**Police** sift the scene with the patience of people who didn’t sign up for performance art. No one is arrested — **yet** — but **everyone** is told things in tones that imply **don’t**.

> **Moses (confessional, Bang trembling in frame):**
> “I’m not rug-pulling them. I’m rug-pulling **myself**. But we’re still solvent in… in **vibes**.”

**Tag**. The cameras find **Nelly** in the IHOP glow, a drip of syrup on one sneaker like a fingerprint.

> **Nelly (to lens, finger raised, preacher calm):**
> “**St. Louis**. Gateway to **Liquidity**. Tours starting at **five dollars**. Bring **cash**.”
