

# FARTWATER — S1E3: New York City

## “Atlantic Antic”

---

### Week 1: Arrival & Setup

The FartWATER convoy crawls into Brooklyn like a busted parade — Luda’s self-funded “luxury” bus in the lead, the FartWATER Van wobbling behind with the decals peeling, and Moses riding shotgun, chain-vaping like a chimney while pounding Bang after Bang. The **Atlantic Antic street fair** is already buzzing with vendors slinging empanadas, Caribbean jerk chicken, and block-long lines for sausage & peppers. Against this backdrop, the crew looks like a circus crash-landing in the middle of real culture.

**Confessional — Moses (dragging a vape):**
“Brooklyn don’t care about your liquidity. Brooklyn *is* liquidity. I’m here with a busted ATM, two scam rappers, and a shoebox of coupons. This is hostile territory.”

---

### The ATM War Begins

Ja Rule insists the **FartWATER Finance BTC ATM** belongs front and center. He chains it down in front of a Caribbean food stand. Moses screams at him in public:

* *“You’re blocking plantains with slippage receipts!”*
* *“This is state fair law! This is NY State Fair!”* Ja yells back, banging the ATM like it’s a pulpit.

The ATM immediately spits:

> **“OUT OF STATE ERROR. SLIPPAGE: 80%. PLEASE RELOCATE.”**

Receipts flutter onto the vendor’s grill. Chicken skewers ignite.

**Confessional — Ja Rule:**
“New York respect the hustle. That’s why I brought the ATM here. This is my city. Moses don’t understand — receipts *are* scripture.”

---

### Bow Wow’s Sbarro Scheme

Meanwhile, Bow Wow drags in two greasy pizza boxes from a **Times Square Sbarro.** He slaps a handwritten sign on a folding table:

> *“Real New York Pizza — Sponsored by FartWATER.”*

Locals stop, laugh, boo. One guy yells, *“That’s mall food, go back to Ohio!”* Bow insists it’s authentic.

**Confessional — Bow Wow (sweating, pizza grease on his FartWATER pajamas):**
“Sbarro’s the heart of the city, man. That’s New York fine dining. These clowns don’t know nothing about utility. Pizza is utility.”

He tries to resell individual slices for $8, “with a free FartWATER.” Nobody buys.

---

### Groupies’ Fake VIP Setup

The groupies rope off a 6-foot section of sidewalk with neon string and cardboard: **“VIP ZONE – $5 Selfies.”** Crystal waves a laminated pass. Diamond starts taking Venmo. Peaches steals a churro from the next stall.

A vendor shouts at them to move. They don’t. Within an hour, three drunk dudes are sitting in the “VIP” eating tacos and flicking receipts at passersby.

---

### Luda’s Megaphone Flex

Luda marches down Atlantic Ave with a megaphone shouting:

* *“Luda still the headliner! You see these buses? That’s me!”*
* Nobody responds.
  He demands someone start a chant. Nobody does.

**Confessional — Luda (red-faced):**
“They acting like I ain’t run this street. I *am* the Antic! The Antic is me! Headliner forever!”

---

### Will & Soulja Arrive

Will Smith and Soulja Boy roll up late, carrying a ring light and a busted Bluetooth speaker. They announce they’ll launch the “NYC Dance Challenge” later in the week. Nobody cares.

**Confessional — Soulja:**
“First rapper to do a Brooklyn dance challenge. Watch this space.”

The stream URL on their flyer? It already redirects to **/bustasfinance/** with a 451 error.

---

### End of Week 1

The episode closes on Moses pacing in front of the chained ATM, receipts in both hands, vape cloud hanging over him.

**Moses (ranting):**
“You don’t anchor liquidity in a food stall! You anchor liquidity under a bridge! This whole fair’s a rug pull!”

> *“Next week: receipts rain on Atlantic Antic.”*

Atlantic Ave is wall-to-wall vendors, bands, and street crowds. The FartWATER camp is jammed between a roti stall and a craft jewelry table. ### Morning: The Receipts Multiply

By Saturday, the BTC ATM has gone feral.

* It spits nonstop receipts, piling knee-high.
* Messages cycle faster, glitchier:

  * **“AUTO SLIPPAGE: 80%.”**
  * **“NEW YORK LIQUIDITY POOL FULL.”**
  * **“PLEASE REMOVE FROM BROOKLYN.”**

Vendors are furious — receipts are blowing into fryers, clogging storm drains, sticking to kids’ shoes.

**Confessional — Moses (vape in hand, eyes twitching):**
“This is what happens when you anchor liquidity in the wrong jurisdiction. You don’t move the ATM, the ATM moves you.”

Ja stands on the ATM, yelling scripture into a megaphone:

* *“This is the new temple! Receipts are commandments!”*
