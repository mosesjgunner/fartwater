**FARTWATER — S1E1: “ATLANTA — RECEIPTS IN THE PEACH STATE”**

*(Trash-docu tone: handheld cameras, overexposed daylight, 2004 MTV graphics fighting with 2025 TikTok filters. Everyone’s mic is too hot, everyone’s ego louder than the generator.)*

**Theme:** *Headliner Energy vs. Actual Permits*
**Hook:** The “FartWATER Finance Tour” launches at the **Georgia State Fair**, but the team’s plan to sell “Hotdog Water Hydration” devolves into a county-fair crime scene of ego, syrup, and fake finance.

---

## PART 1 — COLD OPEN + WEEK 1 (LAUNCH / THE GLIZZY HUSTLE)

### **Scene 1 — “Headliner Energy”**

*Early evening over Perry, Georgia.*
A Ferris wheel turns slow and greasy in the humidity. County-fair lights flicker like dying neon gods. A **dented white van** rattles into the dirt lot, the giant **FartWATER bottle** bolted on top wobbling with every bump, squealing like a sad mascot.

**LUDACRIS** steps out first, wearing sunglasses darker than the situation. He spreads his arms to the empty fairground like it’s the Super Bowl halftime show.

> **Luda (confessional, grainy cam):** “ATL is my city. I bring *headliner energy*. Doesn’t matter if the stage is mud or marble.”

Behind him, **Moses Gunner** emerges from the chaos bus, vaping through a **Bang Energy can** with a bent straw poking out. His lanyard swings with a laminated **Waffle House receipt** labeled in Sharpie: *“PERMIT.”*
**Ja Rule** stumbles down the ramp dragging the cursed **BTC ATM**, metal scraping asphalt like a bad omen.
**Busta Rhymes** wrestles a stack of foam boards labeled **“HSL-401: Hydration Throughput”** like a PowerPoint possessed by demons.

**Bow Wow** exits last — FartWATER pajamas, crusted crown, eating something unidentifiable from a paper bag.

> **Bow (confessional):** “I woke up in gravy. That’s just brand alignment.”

The **FartWATER Girls** arrive like a discount Destiny’s Child:

* **Crystal**, holding a ring light taller than her.
* **Diamond**, wearing a headset that isn’t connected to anything.
* **Peaches**, chewing a hotdog she clearly stole.

They walk in slow motion while the soundtrack skips between *Missy Elliott bass* and *iPhone ringtone*.
The director yells, “We’re rolling!”
**Crystal:** “We’re always rolling.”
**Diamond:** “Rolling is content.”
**Peaches:** “Then I’m a whole movie.”

---

### **Scene 2 — “The Midway Pitch”**

*Daytime chaos.*
A crooked folding table becomes the **FartWATER Finance Booth.** Two trash bags serve as banners. A sign duct-taped to the front reads:

> “BAR-S HOT DOG HUSTLE — WIN $50 FRTWTR + CASE OF FARTWATER™.”

**Moses** duct-tapes a QR code onto a milk crate. It immediately peels off.
**Diamond** hands out “Premium Proximity Wristbands” (shoelaces with Sharpie).
**Luda** poses for imaginary cameras.
**Ja Rule** thumps the ATM, declaring, “Liquidity is live, baby!”
**Bow** insists on entering his own contest. He bites into a lukewarm hotdog, freezes, then looks directly into the lens.

> **Bow:** “I think I just chipped a tooth on the American dream.”

The crowd — five confused fairgoers and one kid in a Braves jersey — starts chanting “EIGHTY PERCENT!” like they know what it means.
**Crystal** records everything vertically, her ring light reflecting in the deep grease sheen of Bow’s forehead.

A fair worker approaches, clipboard trembling: “Y’all got a vendor permit?”
**Moses:** “We got receipts.”
**Ja:** “And faith.”
**Peaches (mouth full):** “And relish.”

The worker retreats, unsure whether to call security or church.

---

### **Scene 3 — “The Glizzy Airdrop”**

The contest starts.
Five volunteers. Three hot plates. One tragic extension cord.
Music: a chopped & screwed remix of *Country Grammar*.

Bow is seated dead center, sweating through his FartWATER hoodie.
Ja Rule, emceeing, shouts from atop a picnic table, waving a bottle like it’s a mic.

> **Ja:** “Who’s ready to *Always Pay Rule*!?”
> **Crowd:** *confused cheering*

The stopwatch app on someone’s phone starts.
The eaters go to war. Mustard sprays. Buns disintegrate. The crowd grows — not in support, but curiosity.
A local teen yells, “Is this TikTok?”
**Crystal:** “It’s *meta* TikTok.”
Nobody knows what that means.

**Bow** pushes through, veins bulging, gravy dripping.
When the timer hits zero, the smallest contestant — a quiet guy in a NASCAR tee — lifts his last dog in triumph.
Ja Rule screams “AIRDROP CONFIRMED!” and throws a FartWATER bottle like a bouquet. It bounces off a stanchion and knocks over the sign.
The bottle hisses open. Peaches catches it mid-spray like it’s champagne at a pyramid scheme.

---

### **Scene 4 — “Birth of FartWATER Gravy™”**

**Aftermath montage.**
Steam from the hotdog pot curls into the night.
**Bow**, eyes glassy, holds up the leftover water like it’s holy.

> **Bow:** “Waste not, want not. This is flavor equity.”

He pours a FartWATER can into the pot. It foams violently.
**Moses** nods, reverent. “We’re watching utility happen in real time.”
**Diamond** scribbles *FARTWATER GRAVY™* on a cardboard scrap.
**Peaches** starts ladling samples into Dixie cups.
**Crystal** films in 240fps slow-mo. Glitter filters. Caption:

> *“New drop. Limited batch. Flavor = redemption.”*

Cut to **Busta**, staring at the pot like it’s a sermon prop.

> **Busta:** “Viscosity equals throughput!”
> He slams his fist on the table. The pot spills onto the dirt.
> The audience screams. Peaches cheers.
> The screen freeze-frames on the boiling mess, text overlay:
> **“HYDRATION HAS NEVER BEEN THIS THICK.”**

### **Scene 5 — “Compliance, But Make It County”**

*Next morning. Golden-hour hangover.*
The fairground is littered with napkins, bottles, and one melted sign that says “APR = Always Pay Rule.”
**Moses** struts up to the county permit booth like it’s Shark Tank. He’s holding a folder of random receipts stapled together with confidence.

**Official:** “These are food orders.”
**Moses:** “They’re also permits, if you zoom out spiritually.”
**Official:** “You can’t sell unregistered beverages.”
**Ja:** “We’re not selling. We’re blessing.”
**Official:** “You can’t bless unregistered beverages either.”
**Busta (behind them):** “Consensus achieved!”

The fair cop shrugs and walks away, muttering “Not my jurisdiction.”
**Crystal** films it as a victory montage, slow-motion with gospel music.
**Diamond** updates her notes app: *“We are legally hydrated.”*
**Peaches** steals a funnel cake from another booth and declares, “Breakfast = yield.”

---

### **Scene 6 — “Headliner Walkthrough”**

Downtown Atlanta.
**Luda** struts through the streets in full stage gear — rhinestone towel, shades, laminated “Headliner” pass. He’s holding a megaphone that nobody asked for.

He announces himself to every block like a lost parade:

> “ATL! Headliner walkthrough! Main stage energy only!”

People sidestep him with practiced Atlanta grace.
A teenager filming on his phone asks, “Who’s that?”
His friend: “Some kinda state fair YouTuber, I think.”

Cutaway confessional:

> **Luda:** “When you’re the headliner, the world moves around you. Unless the world’s on lunch break. Then you wait.”

Behind him, **Peaches** is feeding pigeons FartWATER Gravy™ in a takeout box.
They hiss, then explode into flight like money laundering.

say less — rolling right into **Part 2** and **Part 3** in the same grimy VH1-meets-TikTok tone. no receipt clutter, just picture-painting chaos.

## PART 2 — WEEK 2 + WEEK 3 (SHOW DAY / DENNY’S → WAFFLE → IHOP)

### **Scene 7 — “Load-In (Fairgrounds Stage)”**

Morning heat like a damp towel. The fair stage is plywood with delusions. A fog machine exhales a sickly little sigh, then gives up.
**Will & Soulja** assemble a collapsible pulpit that looks ordained by Craigslist. **Will** tapes note cards to it like scripture; **Soulja** sticks logo stickers over the screws and calls it “compliance.”
**Ja** drags the ATM up a ramp that bows like an apology; **Moses** kisses the chain he uses to lock it in place, whispering “stay.”
**Busta** unrolls **HSL-401** foam boards—arrows, circles, a big word *CONSENSUS* written five times like a summoning. He calibrates a laser pointer that only points at his own chest.
Backstage, the **FartWATER Girls** claim a corner: **Crystal** preps a ring-light glam shot with a background of dented trash cans; **Diamond** does fake check-ins with a spiral notebook; **Peaches** taste-tests anything that isn’t nailed down and declares herself “QA.”

A tech with a mullet and a heart of gold shakes his head at the tangled cables.

> **Tech:** “Y’all want sound or sparks?”
> **Busta (beaming):** “Yes.”

### **Scene 8 — “Soundcheck, Or Something Like It”**

The mics are all hot and somehow whispery. **Luda** paces the empty lawn, treating every ant as an audience member.
**Will** practices his sermon—big eyes, bigger pauses—while **Soulja** interrupts to count imaginary money on beat.
**Busta** tests breath control into the mic; the speakers groan like they’ve met him before.
**Bow** wanders onstage with a paper boat of fries he swears are for “charity research,” then eats half and renames it “loss prevention.”
**Moses** strums the mic cable like a guitar; it hums a note that sounds exactly like regret.

### **Scene 9 — “Gates Open (People Trickle, Ego Floods)”**

The first wave of humanity is carnival kids with tiger face-paint, moms pushing strollers, uncles bargaining for kettle corn like they’re buying a boat.
**Luda** hits an intro like he’s surfacing beneath Coachella: towel cape, slow turn, hand to sky. A single balloon floats by as if to clap once, politely.
He raps. The air tastes like powdered sugar and compromise.
Mid-bar, **Busta** barges in yelling **“CONSENSUS ACHIEVED!”** as confetti coughs out like a smoker in January. Kids chase three lonely pieces.
**Will & Soulja** seize the pulpit. Will’s voice finds the big preacher pitch. Soulja echoes like a hype man for a TED Talk:

> **Will:** “Hydration is family.”
> **Soulja:** “FIIIRST FAMILY HYDRATION!”
> They’re glowing, because delusion is great lighting.

**Ja** gestures to his machine with the gravitas of a christening. An uncle in a visor nods, impressed by nothing and by everything. Behind them, **Crystal** captures it all in vertical 9:16, cutting between shots of **Diamond** selling yarn wristbands and **Peaches** stealing a corn dog with the grace of a raccoon in pearls.

### **Scene 10 — “The Crowd Moment”**

For thirty electric seconds, the lawn *believes.*
Even the generator seems to purr in key.
A teenager hoists a toddler onto his shoulders; the kid claps on the ones that don’t exist.
**Luda** sees the opening and glides into an old hook; the entire fair, for one beat, is 2003 again—sticky, fearless, loud.

Then a funnel-cake fryer burps and pops like it wants royalties. The spell shivers. The sound desk twitches. **Busta** tries to roll a fog burst; it hisses at a sprinkler head in warning. The strobe kicks once, like a camera flash for a mugshot.
From stage right, **Peaches** trips the safety rope on a banner and catches it with one hand, shrugging: “I *meant* to.” The crowd cheers her like a local hero.

### **Scene 11 — “Denny’s, Closed for Comedy”**

Midnight bleeds into fluorescent white. **Denny’s** hums like a hospital waiting room that learned to fry.
The crew piles in, loud with victory they didn’t exactly have. **Luda** asks for the “headliner booth.” The host points to the sidewalk.
**Will** smiles and invites the staff to hydrate and repent. **Soulja** says he invented 24 hours.
A manager in a tie that’s doing its best escorts them backward through the door while nodding “uh-huh, uh-huh, absolutely not.”
On the curb, **Crystal** turns their exile into a slow-mo epic. **Diamond** announces they’ll “circulate capital elsewhere.” **Peaches** holds up a sugar packet like a trophy.
**Bow** takes a bite of nothing and says it tastes like childhood.

### **Scene 12 — “Waffle House (2:13 AM, Syrup & Violence)”**

The lights here are honest; everything is too bright to lie.
**Moses** clutches his chain-worn **FartLedger™** like a baby possum. **Peaches** sees the glint, reaches with a “let me hold it,” and the chain says enough is enough.
Metal scream, little pop. The ledger swings into two halves, like a breakup no one will admit is mutual.
**Busta** spins with a plate of hashbrowns and blesses the room with flying potatoes. **Ja** shepherds the ATM out the back like livestock that knows its own name.
Cooks barely glance up; a waitress refills a coffee until hope overflows.
**Luda** tries to make it a photo op; a line cook drifts into the frame and blots him like a solar eclipse.
**Peaches** wears half the chain like an Olympic medal. **Diamond** applauds, invoices nobody.

### **Scene 13 — “IHOP Council of Elders”**

Blue vinyl booths. Syrup caddies blinking like little beacons. The carpet smells like church and childhood.
**Nelly** fans out coupon sheets like tarot: neat, reverent, a little dangerous. He speaks low, like the IRS might be in the syrup.
**Bow** baptizes a short stack in **FartWATER Gravy™** and names it **Stable Stack** with his mouth full.
**Will** holds both their hands like a guidance counselor. **Soulja** suggests dynamic pricing based on pancake vibes and calls it innovation.
**Moses** opens his shoebox of “treasury” with the pomp of a notary public on reality TV. He arranges paper into a tiny skyline of bad ideas.
**Crystal** films it like a heist movie. **Diamond** sells access to the corner of the booth as “founders’ circle.” **Peaches** pockets two butter packets and a fork because she promised herself a souvenir.
The waitress returns, stone-faced, and comp-slaps exactly one short stack onto the table like a mercy law.

**Nelly** closes his eyes. For a second, he looks like an altar boy who survived every sermon.

> **Nelly:** “Coupons are mercy the government forgot.”
> No one argues. Even the neon sign outside hums amen.

## PART 3 — WEEK 4 + TAG (THE CHASE / AFTERMATH)

### **Scene 14 — “Van-ish (Sirens & Scrap Metal)”**

The night peels back to blue-black. Sirens doppler past like they’re chasing better television.
Everyone pours into the **FartWATER Van**. The giant roof bottle tilts, hopeful, then tips, doubtful.
**Ja** yanks the chained machine inside; it thunks against the ribs of the van like a trapped whale. **Moses** dives after a fistful of papers and comes up with air and a laugh that sounds like a receipt tearing.
**Crystal** films from the passenger seat, adding captions that turn panic into a montage. **Diamond** coaches no one: “Smile with your eyes.”
**Peaches** braces her foot on a milk crate and screams, joyful: “Go, go, go!”

The van lurches, snorts, coughs, remembers it is old, and decides to sit with that. Everything in frame keeps moving except them.

### **Scene 15 — “Tow Theology”**

A tow truck arrives with the patience of a man who has seen every version of this. He hooks the machine first—no ceremony, just competence—and starts rolling.
**Luda** slips into a slow walk beside it like pallbearer meets paparazzi. His towel drapes perfect; the fair lights behind him put a halo on cheap plastic.
**Ja** pours a little FartWATER on the asphalt and murmurs something that could be a prayer or a refund policy.
**Moses** watches the red taillights drag their sin into a smaller and smaller square of possibility. His face rearranges itself into acceptance, then comedy.
**Peaches** blows a kiss at the departing bulk. “Text me!”
**Crystal** gets the shot: wet eyes, cheap lights, beautiful failure.
For two seconds, the music swells like prestige TV. Then a golf cart buzzes through the frame and steals the pathos.

### **Scene 16 — “Confessionals Under Sodium Lights”**

One by one, under a parking-lot lamp that flatters no one:

* **Nelly** cradles a stack of coupons like hymnals. “Bonds, baby. We’ll sing them into value.”
* **Bow** licks syrup off his knuckles, dreamy. “Stable Stack… tastes like a hug in debt.”
* **Busta** spreads his arms at the empty night. “Throughput is just breath with calendars.”
* **Will** smiles soft, damp. “Family is liquidity, and we are drenched.”
* **Soulja** points at the sky. “First rapper to headline the outtakes.”
* **Moses** laughs without teeth. “I didn’t rug them. I just finally caught up to the floor.”

Cutaway: **FartWATER Girls** huddled on the van’s bumper.
**Crystal** edits the recap in-app, adding sparkles to catastrophe.
**Diamond** lists “Founders’ Access Wristbands (Unredeemable)” on a resale app with a shipping fee that could buy lunch.
**Peaches** sells the last stolen churro to a kid in a Spider-Man hoodie and calls him “investor.”

### **Scene 17 — “Tag — Gateway to Liquidity”**

Dawn is the same color as the cheap coffee they didn’t finish. The convoy crawls onto the highway; the roof bottle wobbles, gallant.
**Nelly** rides shotgun, coupons clipped to the visor like prayer flags. He looks into the lens with the calm of a man who picked a religion and found it was breakfast.

> **Nelly:** “St. Louis. Five-dollar tours. Bring cash.”
> Hard cut to a postcard of the **Gateway Arch** catching first light.
> A single piece of confetti—old, damp, hopeful—floats across the frame and sticks to the camera. Smash to black.
