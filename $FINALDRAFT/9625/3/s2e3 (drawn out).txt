## Bow’s SBARRO Sponsorship — How it works (and why it’s funny)

*(Answering “WHAT DO YOU KNOW ABOUT SPONSORSHIP??” and baking it into the gag.)*

**What Bow strong-arms Sbarro into (in-story, real mechanics under the comedy):**

* **Type:** **In-kind** (free product) + **contra deal** (Bow’s posts & stage shoutouts in return).
* **Deliverables Bow promises:**

  1. 3 TikToks/week tagging Sbarro (food court POV + “Pizza Plan” bundle).
  2. On-stage shout during Hydration Minutes (“powered by pizza”).
  3. Logo **placement** on his self-printed lanyard/backdrop (lol).
  4. Inclusion in Crystal’s recap (“Official Pizza Partner”) — truth-stretch.
* **What he extracts:** 2 sheet pies per show + fountain drinks; during the spike, it upgrades to unlimited soda (no lids) because the *live* is cooking.
* **Why a real sponsor would even nibble:** he shows **live concurrent viewers** + **foot-traffic** proof (mall cam shots) + **UGC of teens** eating the Pizza Plan. That’s a legit **impressions** pitch, even if chaotic.
* **Bow’s “deck” (trash but functional):**

  * **Audience:** mall teens (13–19) + nostalgia dads (30–40).
  * **Reach:** “Crystal’s live avg 18k CCV, spikes 80k+ on #TraumaShake.”
  * **Assets:** stage plug, 3 posts, 1 recap feature, POS tent card that says “Authentic NY Pizza (from your local mall).”
  * **Rights:** 30-day social repost rights for Sbarro, no paid media spend.
  * **Exclusivity:** **Food-court pizza exclusive** (Bow can still cheat on them outside the mall).
* **How it breaks:** Bow floods the zone with free slices to appease dropped calls → costs explode → Sbarro corp calls mall ops → **sponsorship “paused”** (but Bow already made the content).

> TL;DR sponsorship types we’re spoofing: **cash**, **in-kind**, **contra**, **category exclusivity**, **usage rights**, **deliverables/impressions**, and a fake **rate card** (Bow’s tears = value).
